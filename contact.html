06 / A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS
Before you ask,
you might wonder.
A few things to include so your enquiry starts in the right place.
01Does this form confirm my table?+
No. It prepares a request you can copy or download. Contact the restaurant through a confirmed channel and wait for the team to confirm availability.
02What should I mention about food allergies?+
List specific allergens and the severity of each allergy. Discuss cross-contact and preparation directly with the team before ordering; a note alone does not establish that a dish is suitable.
03Can I enquire for a larger group?+
Yes. Select “A group occasion” and include a rough headcount, preferred date, budget range, and any seating or menu preferences you want to discuss.
04What if I need to change an existing booking?+
Use the contact details provided with your booking confirmation. Include the name, date, and reference so the team can identify your plans.
05Can I ask about access or a quieter table?+
Absolutely. Describe what would make the visit more comfortable. Ask the team to confirm the available facilities and seating before you travel.
