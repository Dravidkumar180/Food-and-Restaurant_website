    BEFORE YOU TAKE THE NEXT STEP

    A few things
    worth clearing up.

    What happens after I review my request?

    Choose Continue to demo payment to try a simulated checkout and see a demo confirmation. You can also copy or download the request. No real order is placed.

    Will I be charged on this page?

    No. The payment page simulates a card, UPI, or pay-at-restaurant flow. A real payment provider is required before live transactions can be offered.

    Are taxes and delivery included in this estimate?

    The estimate contains the listed menu items only. Any applicable taxes, packaging, service or delivery charges must be confirmed before you accept a final total.

    What should I mention about an allergy?

    List the specific allergen and discuss preparation and cross-contact directly with the restaurant. A note alone does not confirm that a dish is suitable.
